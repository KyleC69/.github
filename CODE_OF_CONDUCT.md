# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this community to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone.
Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good intent, and help keep collaboration productive.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that help create a positive environment include:
- being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews
- giving and receiving feedback in a constructive way
- staying focused on the technical issue being discussed
- showing empathy toward other community members
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- personal attacks, insults, or inflammatory language
- trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith participation
- publishing private or sensitive information without permission
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention
- encouraging or engaging in abusive conduct

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct and may take any action they deem appropriate, including temporary or permanent restrictions on participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately through the security or support channels described in this repository.
